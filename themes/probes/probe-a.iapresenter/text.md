# Broadsheet

#### A signature theme for talk-first decks

E1 — if this renders in Broadsheet (heavy serif hero, near-black background, red accent), then `"template": "broadsheet"` in `info.json` resolves a custom theme by its lowercased `Name`. If it falls back to the default look, try `"Broadsheet"`, then rename the folder — see `themes/README.md`.

This deck doubles as the theme's showcase: every surface it styles appears once.

---

# The queue is not the problem

#### It never was

The hero at full scale, with a qualifier under it. This is the slide the whole theme is built around — one claim, one qualifier, nothing else competing. The heading should read as roughly a fifth of the slide's height.

---

## Ninety seconds is the whole product

	Body text sits at about 5.6% of slide height, which is inside the legible band for a room at normal viewing distance.

	One **bold phrase** per slide takes the accent. That is the only emphasis allowed — if everything is emphasised, nothing is.

Notice the left-flush alignment and the generous margin. Editorial, not broadcast.
